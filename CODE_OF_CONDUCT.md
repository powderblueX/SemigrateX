# Code of conduct

SemigrateX contributors should communicate with respect and focus on the technical issue. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation after repeated or serious violations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Include links or dates where possible. The maintainer will acknowledge a report when a private contact channel is available and will avoid publishing the reporter's identity without consent.
